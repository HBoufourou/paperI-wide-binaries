# Status of the historical DR4 protocol — 8 September 2026

`PREREGISTRATION_DR4.md` is retained with its original bytes and history. This dated note does not retrospectively alter its registration date or criteria.

The catalogue-provenance error documented in the root [correction](../CORRECTION.md) invalidates the affected observational premises of the earlier paper. The current replacement is a conditional simulation study with its own declared [scientific protocol](../corrected-2026-09-08/work/final_paper/PROTOCOL.md).

Its new controls do not demonstrate that real Gaia DR4 contamination, selection and astrometric products satisfy the simulator. The historical protocol must not be cited as evidence that a decisive DR4 gravity test has already been validated. Any future change of the real-data analysis plan must be dated and documented separately.
