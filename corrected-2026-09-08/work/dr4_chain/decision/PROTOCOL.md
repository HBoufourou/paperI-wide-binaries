# Conditional decision engine for the coupled gravity/astrometry chain

This protocol is specified before any new coupled-library decision outcomes. Earlier DR4 experiments, including failed transfer and finite-library limitations, motivated this design. It is prospective within a post-inspection development project, not a preregistration or human-blind experiment. No older science is modified. Physical orbit generation and validation belong to the separate upstream pipeline.

The upstream population conditions are those of ../POPULATION_PROTOCOL.md as amended by ../AMENDMENT_01_PHOTOMETRIC_ANCHOR.md: true mass is set from a fixed empirical total photometric-mass anchor before orbital scaling, and observed separation includes the initial photocentre offset. The prior revision and the seed-only decision amendment AMENDMENT_01_TEST_SEEDS.md both precede physical decision outcomes. Neither changes this engine's score, representation, nuisance grid or decision threshold.

## Scope and measured interface

The input manifest describes fresh fitting, calibration and test libraries for two gravity hypotheses (Newton, QUMOND), pure systems and companion-prior locations 40/80 AU. Test-only libraries additionally use actual 60/160 AU priors. These numbers name locations of a physical prior before admissibility rejection; they are not mixture-weight labels or retained medians. The external stationary-population nuisance is beta=1 or1.6. A beta=1.3 importance law is tested separately outside the fitted nuisance grid, using only test-role libraries.

Each library record has gravity, role, component, prior_au, population_path, observations{scenario:path}, metadata and hashes. Component is pure, comp40, comp80, comp60 or comp160. Population contains measured rproj_kau[N,2] (source2−source1 in RA*/Dec axes), distance_pc, Mphot1, Mphot2, and nonnegative weight_beta1/weight_beta1p6/weight_beta1p3 arrays. Each observation file contains pm34_masyr/pm66_masyr[N,2], cov66_masyr2/cov_delta_relative_masyr2[N,2,2], pm_source34_masyr/pm_source66_masyr[N,2,2], cov_delta_source_masyr2[N,2,2,2], and source residual/acceleration scores at both baselines[N,2]. Only these measured fields enter the feature function. Gravity/companion labels and energy weights select simulated training/calibration/test populations; they are never predictors of individual systems.

Libraries must use the same declared admissibility and observational parent selection before weights are normalized separately within each component. The mixture f is the fraction of these admissible parents. No diagnostic cut or velocity rejection is added by this engine. For N=1000 parents, every supplied measured row contributes to a bin. Upstream selection, mass/flux assumptions, role independence and the empirical covariate reuse remain explicit conditions. The source manifest must authorize production after its physical validation; absent authorization only clearly marked software fixtures can run. No real-Gaia-ready inference follows from this switch.

## Frozen joint representation

For each parent, let s be the magnitude of rproj_kau, Mphot=Mphot1+Mphot2, vN=[sqrt(GM_SUN/AU)/1000] sqrt(Mphot/(1000s)) km/s, and v66=4.740470463533349 distance_pc pm66_masyr/1000 km/s. Project v66 onto the observed separation unit vector and its counterclockwise perpendicular, then divide both signed components by vN. The separation coordinate is s/rM(Mphot), with rM=sqrt(GM_SUN Mphot/a0), a0=1.2e−10 m/s², GM_SUN=1.3271244e20 m³/s² and AU=149597870700 m. These constants match the upstream physical integrator. The formal precision coordinate is sqrt(trace(cov66)/2) times the PM-to-km/s factor divided by vN.

The continuous diagnostic score is the maximum over all source residual/acceleration scores at34/66 months, both source PM-change Mahalanobis scores, and the relative PM-change Mahalanobis score. Covariances describe the shared66−34 measurement difference, not the sum of independent-release covariances. The full observable joint histogram has:

- 4 separation classes, edges[0,0.3,1,3,infinity].
- 8 classes for each signed velocity component, edges[−infinity,−sqrt(2),−1,−0.5,0,0.5,1,sqrt(2),infinity].
- 4 diagnostic classes, edges[0,3,10,100,infinity], equivalently thresholds on the monotone log1p score.
- 2 precision classes, split at0.05.

There are2048 joint cells. These choices are fixed without reading new held-out distributions. Both velocities and diagnostics are coarsened; the engine does not claim a lossless continuous likelihood. Distance and photometric mass enter observable conversions/normalization, and formal velocity precision is jointly binned. No independent-marginal likelihood factors are introduced. The same parent indices are used for all three cadence scenarios and all hypothesis comparisons.

Invalid/nonfinite observables, nonpositive separation/distance/mass, inconsistent shapes, nonsymmetric/nonpositive covariance, missing declared scenario, unsupported catalogue size or missing bank/physics provenance cause an explicit unsupported/indeterminate result. Finite overflow bins do not establish valid physical extrapolation. Absolute simulation adequacy, raw empty-bin fractions, weight concentration and the stated physical domain are reported. Passing a calibrated score cannot certify an untested nuisance value, an unmodelled selection or an invalid force interpolation.

## Weighted templates and discrete nuisance profiling

For each gravity, beta, scenario and fitting component, normalize weights over its admissible rows and form the2048-cell probability vector. Define n_eff=1/sum(w²). Apply a symmetric Dirichlet total concentration1: p=(n_eff p_empirical+1/2048)/(n_eff+1). This makes logarithms finite; it does not create physical support. Record effective counts, maximum weights, nonempty bins and added probability mass. No smoothing strength is tuned using calibration or test outcomes.

The mixture is p=(1−f)p_pure+f[(1−eta)p_comp40+eta p_comp80]. Profile beta∈{1,1.6}, f∈{0,0.075,0.15,0.225,0.30} and eta∈{0,0.5,1}, retaining only eta=0 when f=0. These are26 unique nuisance points per gravity. Eta is a mixture fraction, not a physical median. Rank calibration protects the declared discrete family of calibrating empirical laws when a new catalogue and calibration references are exchangeable under that same law. Transfer to a separately generated finite test library has additional library Monte Carlo error and is evaluated empirically; it is not automatically covered by that finite-sample rank guarantee. No guarantee for nuisance interpolation is asserted.

For counts n_b summing to1000, compute multinomial deviance D_h=min_theta 2 sum_b n_b log[n_b/(1000 p_h,theta,b)], with zero-count terms zero. This is used as a simulation score, without an asymptotic chi-square assumption. The relative score is R_h=D_h−D_other. Both statistics are recomputed after nuisance profiling on every catalogue. Save best-fitting nuisance indices and near-degenerate alternatives; nuisance parameters may absorb genuine gravity differences, which appropriately reduces decision power.

## Independent composite calibration and decisions

For each true hypothesis and each of its26 nuisance points, draw1000 catalogues of1000 parents from fresh calibration-role weighted libraries. Use the same selected parent indices for the three cadence observations. This produces156000 calibration catalogue views. Drawing component and row indices with replacement is exact for the finite weighted library; no assumption that separate cadence histograms are independent is made.

For each hypothesis h, each statistic A∈{D_h,R_h}, and each calibration nuisance theta, set p_h,theta,A=(1+#calibration A>=observed A)/(1000+1). Define the composite marginal p_h,A=max_theta p_h,theta,A and the combined p_h=min(1,2 min(p_h,D,p_h,R)). The maximum protects the discrete nuisance union of calibrating laws; the factor2 is a Bonferroni combination of adequacy and relative evidence. Correlation between these statistics is allowed. Conditional on separate finite calibration and test pools, catalogue draws from those pools are not exactly exchangeable. The independent test therefore measures this transfer, including finite-library discrepancy, rather than inheriting an automatic exact p-value guarantee.

Reject h only when p_h<=0.01. Return Newton when QUMOND is rejected and Newton retained, QUMOND for the converse, otherwise indeterminate. Distinguish both_retained, both_rejected and unsupported reasons. There is no forced answer, no selection of a verdict by highest likelihood alone, and no threshold change after test results. Wrong-classification control under a calibrated true hypothesis is distinct from correct-classification power and from adequacy of the physical model.

## Test cells, seeds and reporting

Main tests cross gravity2, beta{1,1.6}, prior{40,80,60,160}, f{0,0.075,0.225,0.30},1000 catalogues and3 cadences:192000 views. Additional declared beta1.3 tests use prior{60,160},f{0,0.30},both gravities and all cadences:24000 views. Overall there are216000 test views, with60/160 and beta1.3 outside-family cases labelled separately. The f=0 entries under different named inner priors are additional random repetitions of the same pure physical population, not independent physical prior tests. No additional stress case is chosen after results.

Calibration seed=270000001+1000000*gravity_index+10000*beta_index+100*nuisance_mixture_index, where gravity indices are0/1, beta indices0/1 and mixture indices0..12 in ascending f then eta order. Test seed=274000001+3000000*gravity_index+10000*beta_index+1000*prior_index+100*f_case_index, with beta indices0/1/2 for1/1.6/1.3; prior indices0/1/2/3 for40/80/60/160; f-case indices0/1/2/3 for0/.075/.225/.30. Only the declared beta1.3 combinations execute. These124 scientific streams are distinct and shared across cadence views deliberately. Software-only fixtures use279000001 onwards and never enter scientific evaluation. Newton tests use274M and QUMOND tests use277M. The already-executed independent analytic fixture275000001 remains reserved and unchanged. AMENDMENT_01_TEST_SEEDS.md records the pre-production correction; seed_audit.py checks all124 scientific streams against declared upstream generation and validation streams.

Report each individual cell: correct/wrong/indeterminate counts, both-retained/both-rejected, true-hypothesis retention, empirical rates and Wilson95% intervals, nuisance degeneracy, raw empty-bin support and conditional weight concentration. Paired cadence differences use the same catalogue-index rows. Operational count targets are correct>=95% and wrong<=1%; a separate stronger confidence target is Wilson lower(correct)>=95% and Wilson upper(wrong)<=1%. Never conflate observed rates with those confidence statements or average away a failing cell. These are marginal Monte Carlo summaries conditional on finite libraries, not simultaneous confidence bounds across all cells or independent evidence over complete-library regeneration.

Fresh test observations may share empirical covariates with fitting inputs, but must have independently generated latent phases/noise by role, with provenance recorded. Repeated catalogue draws reuse those finite libraries. N=1000 is fixed; these results do not justify extrapolation to a larger N. A successful software toy must be labelled software_only and does not validate any physical model or actual Gaia decision.
