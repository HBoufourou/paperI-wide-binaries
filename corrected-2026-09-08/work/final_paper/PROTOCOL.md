# Final methodological strengthening experiment

This prospective development protocol follows inspection of the dr4_chain experiment. It is not a preregistration, a blind human experiment, or a claim that the earlier test was never inspected. All earlier files and releases remain unchanged. New physical banks and new catalogue streams are generated only after this document and the new sources are frozen. Results, including failed targets, enter the replacement manuscript without outcome-driven changes to thresholds, methods or test cases.

## Questions and scope

The experiment asks (i) which information blocks account for conditional gravity discrimination, (ii) whether an envelope over independently generated calibration libraries improves transfer to new physical libraries, and (iii) what claims can be supported in a reproducible methodological paper. The scalar Newton/QUMOND model, synthetic external field, restricted stationary parent, empirical covariate pool, photocentre model, selection, inner-monopole approximation and three observation scenarios remain exactly those of work/dr4_chain. This is not a new validation of the Gaia sky or a complete QUMOND three-body solution.

The original fitting libraries remain fixed. Two fresh calibration realizations and two fresh test realizations are generated independently. Consequently the experiment measures calibration/test-library transfer conditional on the fixed fitting libraries; it does not measure variation under rebuilding the entire physical fitting/calibration/test pipeline.

## Fitting family and information variants

All variants use the same original six fitting populations and beta grid{1,1.3,1.6}. This deliberate prospective expansion addresses a weakness revealed in the preceding experiment. The selected companion fraction grid remains{0,.075,.15,.225,.30}; the comp40/comp80 mixture eta remains{0,.5,1}, with only eta=0 at f=0. There are39 nuisance points per gravity. Mixture construction, total-concentration1 smoothing and effective-count normalization retain their previous definitions. The marginal variants are exact projections of the corresponding full representation, including its smoothing. The 34-month representation is built with its own measured covariance and diagnostic fields.

Five fixed variants are compared on the same parent catalogue indices:

1. full66: original2048-bin joint representation using both signed velocity components at66months, normalized separation, continuous diagnostic combining34/66 and differences, and formal66-month precision.
2. no_diagnostics66: exact512-bin projection removing the diagnostic dimension from full66. This isolates the diagnostic information block, with all remaining quantities unchanged.
3. full34:2048-bin representation with measured PM34, covariance34, and only source residual/acceleration diagnostics34. No66-month PM, covariance, residual, acceleration or cross-release difference field is read. It measures the additional information package supplied by the66-month analysis, not the effect of baseline duration in isolation. This is a simulated34-month reference using diagnostics available inside the observer, not a replication of the public Gaia DR3 products.
4. covariates_only66: exact8-bin projection retaining only normalized separation and formal66-month precision. This diagnoses information supplied by the assumed parent and selection; it is not a null expectation of zero physical discrimination and not a test for leaked gravity labels.
5. conditional66: use the full66 mixture distribution, normalize it after mixture construction to obtain p(velocities,diagnostic | separation_class,precision_class), and use conditional multinomial deviance D=2 sum n log[n/(n_z p(y|z))]. There are8 conditioning strata z. Compute this score for each nuisance point before profiling; subtracting two independently minimized full/marginal scores is not equivalent and is prohibited. Zero-count terms contribute0. This removes the direct marginal likelihood contribution of z, but does not equalize the empirical z counts across gravity hypotheses or guarantee independence of power from the parent. It is not an exactly standardized-population experiment, nor a test calibrated conditionally at each possible observed n_z. Normalizing each component before mixing would change the model and is prohibited.

Each variant profiles nuisance parameters separately for each gravity, computing D_h and R_h=D_h-D_other. Counts per catalogue remain N=1000. Neither latent gravity labels, companion truth, E, Lz nor true masses enter measured features. Parameter labels and weights define supervised templates and known simulation populations legitimately.

## Baseline and library-envelope calibration

Calibration realization b=0 is the original independent calibration population pool; b=1,2 are the two fresh pools. Catalogue draws are new under this protocol, including those from b=0. For each b,gravity,nuisance case, draw1000 catalogues of1000 parents with the same indices across variants and cadences. All variants recompute their own score on every catalogue. The references comprise3*2*39*1000*3=702,000 paired catalogue views, evaluated in five variants.

For each library b, keep the previously declared construction: p_h,b,A=max_theta (1+#A_cal>=A_obs)/(1000+1) for A in{D,R}; then p_h,b=min(1,2 min_A p_h,b,A). The baseline uses p_h,0. The new envelope is explicitly p_h,envelope=max_b p_h,b, AFTER combination within each library. It is not the alternative max over b performed separately for each statistic. Reject h only when its chosen p<=.01. Return a gravity only when that gravity is retained and the alternative rejected; otherwise preserve both-retained/both-rejected indeterminate outcomes.

The envelope protects a finite union of calibrating empirical laws when a future catalogue follows one of them. Fresh finite test libraries are not exactly those laws. Its transfer is measured, not assumed exact. No claim of uniform frequentist control over continuous nuisance families, all physical-library realizations or real Gaia is made. All five variants use identical calibration logic. Both baseline and envelope decisions are reported; the envelope is the prospective proposed method and is not selected after comparing outcomes.

## Reserved tests and reporting

In EACH fresh test realization, each gravity is crossed with beta{1,1.3,1.6}, a pure case counted once, and f{.075,.225,.30} crossed with priors{40,80,60,160}:39cases per gravity. Additional outside-grid beta{1.15,1.45} cases use pure and f=.30 with priors60/160:6cases per gravity. This gives90 cases per realization,180 total and540 scenario-specific cells. Each has1000 catalogue draws, N=1000. The540,000 catalogue views are paired across the five variants and two calibration rules, rather than independent new physical evidence per score.

Catalogue streams are enumerated in the frozen statistical declaration. They are distinct from all physical and historical streams. Indices are saved once per case and shared; full profiles, marginal/combined p-values, decisions and reasons are saved. Count arrays may be reconstructed from saved indices and codes, with canonical hashes retained instead of duplicate large count files.

For every cell report correct,wrong,indeterminate,both-retained,both-rejected,true-retained, Wilson95% intervals, support/weight diagnostics and nuisance profile summaries. Targets are observed correct>=95%,wrong<=1%,true-retained>=99%, with separate stronger tests using Wilson lower(correct)>=95%,upper(wrong)<=1%,lower(true-retained)>=99%. Classification and true-model retention are separate gates. These are marginal descriptive gates, not simultaneous guarantees over selected worst cells. No failed cell is replaced by a group mean.

Report paired discordant counts and changes in correct/wrong/indeterminate rates for (full66,no_diagnostics66), (full66,full34), (full66,covariates_only66), (full66,conditional66), and baseline versus envelope within each variant. Do not claim universal superiority or a gain from actual DR4. The two test realizations permit an observed check of library sensitivity; they do not certify convergence over an unlimited sequence of library rebuilds.
