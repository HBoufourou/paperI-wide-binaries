# Final statistical experiment

This is a new prospective branch of a conditional wide-binary gravity benchmark. Earlier physical and statistical releases remain unchanged. The controlling scientific plan is `../PROTOCOL.md`; `STATISTICAL_DECLARATION.json` fixes this branch's numerical sources,414 case streams and all prespecified scenarios. The old experiment informed its design and is development evidence, not a fresh confirmation.

The five information variants are `full66`, `no_diagnostics66`, `full34`, `covariates_only66` and `conditional66`. Every catalogue is shared across variants and the baseline/library-envelope rules. Classification and true-model-retention criteria are separate. Two fresh test-library realizations check specified transfer; they do not establish real-Gaia readiness or convergence over every possible library realization.

Start with:

- `results/REPORT.md`: scientific interpretation and scope of the completed outputs.
- `results/metrics.csv`: every one of540 physical/cadence cells under five variants and two rules.
- `results/method_summary.csv`, `family_summary.csv` and `realization_summary.csv`: separate groups and their extrema.
- `results/confidence_failures.csv`, `count_failures.csv`, `worst_cells.csv`: all failed criteria and exact worst-case identities.
- `results/paired.csv`, `information_summary.csv`, `envelope_effects.csv` and `envelope_summary.csv`: matched comparisons including adverse changes.
- `results/library_support.csv` and `fitting_component_support.csv`: finite-library concentration and support diagnostics, not guarantees for future sample sizes.
- `OUTPUT_CONTRACT.md`: exact arrays, axes, formulas, measured-only input interface and limitations.
- `REPRODUCING.md`: dependencies, verification and separate-directory replay commands.

The13 light software checks are in `test_engine.py`. Saved executed evidence includes `software_fixture/VERIFICATION.json`, `../review/software_fixture_verification.json` and `SEED_AUDIT.json`. The physical-bank evidence is `../review/physical_banks_verification.json`. The independent scientific reconstruction, once complete, is `../review/statistics_verification.json`; read its explicit `complete` and `passed` fields rather than inferring completion from the presence of this README. The independent implementation is distinct from external human peer review.

`engine.py` and `features.py` remain frozen after declaration. Reporting scripts describe saved results without changing profiles, probabilities, thresholds or decisions. Raw observed features exclude latent truth; population energy and parameter labels are used only to define known simulated laws and evaluation truth. The conditional API always returns `sky_ready=False`.
